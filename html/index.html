01 / MSc Dissertation

Event-Driven Simulation of Capacity Management and Care Pathways for Dog Rehoming

A discrete-event simulation of Dogs Trust's rehoming operations, built on 134,645 records extracted from their Salesforce CRM under a formal data sharing agreement. Five operational scenarios were modelled to find which combinations of changes produce the greatest improvement in throughput and welfare outcomes.

  • Python
  • SimPy
  • Salesforce CRM
  • Discrete-Event Simulation
  • GDPR
Status Publication pending — results available on request
Findings & methodology

Key findings

  • Long-term dogs are just 7.3% of the population but consume 40.6% of all kennel days at roughly 13× the per-dog resource cost — a composition problem, not a capacity problem.
  • Combined foster expansion and longest-stay prioritisation rehomes approximately 286 additional dogs per year, reducing mean length of stay by 5.50 days (p < 0.001).
  • Three operational data gaps were documented — including a corrupted Dog ID field and missing adoption end dates — resolved through sensitivity analysis rather than silent adjustment.

Partnership & supervision

Conducted in partnership with Dogs Trust's Behaviour Services Research Team; supervised by Dr Jon Bielby, Liverpool John Moores University. Publication pending Dogs Trust confirmation — results available on request.

02 / Machine Learning

Human Activity Recognition

A six-class classification project identifying human activities from raw smartphone accelerometer data across 36 participants. Finished 3rd on the private leaderboard in a cohort competition by investing in feature engineering rather than model complexity.

  • Python
  • LightGBM
  • scikit-learn
  • Feature Engineering
  • GroupKFold
GitHub repository View repository
Findings & methodology

Approach

  • Engineered 279 features from raw 20 Hz signals across four domains: time-domain statistics, jerk derivatives, FFT frequency-band energy, and cross-axis correlations.
  • GroupKFold cross-validation kept all snippets from the same participant in a single fold, preventing data leakage from individual movement style.

Reported results

  • Score improved from 78.58% to 85.96% through feature engineering alone.
  • Private score 0.90888, public score 0.91329.

03 / NLP & Data Engineering

Reddit Sentiment Analysis

An end-to-end dual-pipeline NLP system analysing public sentiment across Reddit communities, combining a batch pipeline for historical data and a real-time streaming pipeline with a live dashboard refreshing every 100 seconds.

  • Python
  • PRAW
  • MongoDB
  • VADER
  • TextBlob
  • Plotly
  • Hadoop
GitHub repository View repository
Findings & methodology

Pipelines

  • Batch: 5,000+ comments across 6 subreddits, stored in MongoDB with text cleaning, tokenisation, and parallel VADER + TextBlob scoring.
  • Streaming: PRAW live stream → real-time sentiment → interactive Plotly dashboard with rolling averages and distributions, refreshing every 100 seconds.

Observation

Treated model disagreement as signal rather than noise — VADER and TextBlob disagreed in ways that exposed fundamental differences in how each handles social media language.

05 / Stochastic Simulation

Rise of the Superbugs

A stochastic population dynamics study applying branching process theory to model the survival and extinction of antibiotic-resistant bacterial populations across five offspring distributions. Fully reproducible R Markdown report.

  • R
  • R Markdown
  • Monte Carlo
  • Branching Processes
GitHub repository View repository
Findings & methodology

Model-based findings

  • 1,000 independent trajectories per experiment; estimated extinction probability at 0.329 (95% CI [0.2999, 0.3581]).
  • The m = 1 threshold is a critical boundary — moving from m = 1.0 to m = 1.645 shifts extinction probability from 98.9% to 32.9%.
  • Initial population size matters independently of m — extinction probability drops from 34.2% at Z₀ = 1 to effectively zero at Z₀ = 10.

Notes

These are model-based findings from simulated branching processes, not direct clinical predictions. Co-authored with Imariabe Enobakhare.

06 / Independent Research

Unsafe Products and Public Health in Nigeria

What can the public verify about NAFDAC's regulatory results, 2020–2026?

A self-initiated, multi-phase evidence audit of NAFDAC — Nigeria's primary regulatory authority — asking what the public record can actually confirm about enforcement activity, product quality, and health outcomes from January 2020 to September 2026. Conducted under a personal Independent Research Framework enforcing strict separation of evidence collection from interpretation.

  • Independent Research
  • Evidence Audit
  • Public Health
  • Nigeria
  • Regulatory Analysis
Research report · Google Drive Read the report
Findings & scope

Key findings as reported

  • Of 23 interventions reviewed, only 1 has an independently verifiable outcome — 0 show a measured improvement in product quality or health.
  • ~25% medicine failure rate in the two strongest independent studies; specific deaths from unsafe products documented; national prevalence stated as unknown.
  • Central finding: the public evidence is not strong enough to show how much of NAFDAC's work is succeeding, where, and why — an accountability gap, not a verdict on effectiveness.

Scope note

This audit examines the quality and limits of publicly available evidence. It does not conclude that the regulator is ineffective, and it does not claim national prevalence figures beyond what the cited independent studies report.

Research spotlight

Capacity, care, and 134,645 records.

For the MSc dissertation, Amiola built a discrete-event simulation of Dogs Trust's rehoming operations — one of the UK's largest animal welfare charities — using operational data extracted from their Salesforce CRM under a formal data sharing agreement, with GDPR-compliant handling throughout.

Rather than asking “do we have enough kennels?”, the model tested a harder question: which combination of operational changes actually moves outcomes? Five scenarios were simulated, from staffing shifts to foster-network expansion.
